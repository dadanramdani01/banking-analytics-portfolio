# Research Plan
## Business Problem

Marketing campaigns in banking are often inefficient due to poor customer targeting, resulting in low conversion rates and unnecessary operational costs. Banks frequently contact customers without fully understanding which profiles are more likely to respond positively, leading to wasted resources and reduced campaign effectiveness.

---

## Project Objective

This project aims to identify the key factors influencing deposit subscription success and optimize future marketing strategies using customer analytics, inferential statistics, and customer segmentation.

The analysis focuses on understanding customer responsiveness, identifying high-conversion customer profiles, and improving campaign efficiency through data-driven targeting strategies.

---

## Research Plan

## Business Questions

* Which customer segment is most likely to subscribe to deposits?
* Which marketing channel performs best?
* How can campaign effectiveness be improved?

---

## Research Questions (RQ)

### RQ1

Does customer balance affect deposit subscription?

### RQ2

Does previous campaign outcome influence conversion?

### RQ3

Does contact frequency affect marketing success?

### RQ4

Which customer profile has the highest conversion probability?

---

## Hypothesis

### H1

Customers with higher balances are more likely to subscribe.

### H2

Successful previous campaign outcomes increase conversion probability.

### H3

Excessive campaign contact reduces effectiveness.

---

## Statistical Method

| Research Question                      | Method              |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Balance effect on deposit subscription | Independent T-Test  |
| Previous campaign outcome influence    | Chi-Square Test     |
| Contact frequency effect               | ANOVA               |
| Conversion probability factors         | Logistic Regression |

---

## Expected Business Impact

The analysis can improve campaign targeting, reduce marketing waste, and increase customer conversion rates through more efficient and personalized marketing strategies.

---

## KPI / Success Metric

* Increased targeting accuracy
* Better customer segmentation
* Higher campaign efficiency insights
* Improved deposit conversion understanding
